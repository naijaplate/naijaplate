          Privacy and terms

          Privacy Policy

          Last updated: 22 November 2026

          1. About NaijaPlate

          NaijaPlate is a calorie tracker and meal planner for adults aged 18 and over. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean NaijaPlate.

          2. What stays on your device

          The things you enter, such as foods, water, weight, your plan details (age, height, weight, activity, goal), diet choices, and your free trial or Premium status, are saved in your own browser on your own device. We do not receive this information on a server and we cannot see it.

          3. What we may receive

          • Your email, only if you join the waitlist. It goes to the form service we use to collect it.
          • Payment details, only if you subscribe. Payments are handled by Paystack. We do not see or store your card details. We may see your name, email, and payment status so we can send your access code.
          • Messages you send us by email, call, or WhatsApp.
          • Visit counts, if we switch on a privacy-focused analytics tool, so we can see how many people use the site.

          4. How we use it

          To run the site, send your Premium access code, reply to your messages, and send updates you asked for. We do not sell your personal information.

          5. Services we use

          We use hosting (Netlify), payments (Paystack), and a form service for the waitlist. These providers handle data under their own policies.

          6. Your choices

          You can delete the data on your device any time with the "Delete all saved data" button on the Progress tab, or by clearing your browser data. To remove your email from our list, or to ask what we hold about you, contact us at .

          7. Security

          We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website can promise perfect security. Because your entries stay on your device, keep your phone secure.

          8. Children

          NaijaPlate is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

          9. Changes and contact

          We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law. Questions? Contact us at .

          Terms of Use

          Last updated: 22 November 2026

          1. Not medical advice

          NaijaPlate gives general guidance based on standard formulas and average food values. Calories, macros, gram amounts, meal plans, and weight projections are estimates. They are not medical advice. Speak to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or food allergy, or have a history of disordered eating. If you have an allergy, always check ingredients and labels yourself.

          2. Who can use it

          You must be 18 or older to use NaijaPlate.

          3. Free and Premium

          The tracker, planner, weight log, water tracker, and progress chart are free. The weekly meal plan with diet options, grocery list, and weekly report are Premium. You can try Premium free for days. After that, Premium costs per month or per year. Prices may change, and we will show the current price on this page.

          4. Payments and access codes

          Payments are processed by Paystack. After you pay, we send you a personal access code to unlock Premium. Please do not share or resell your code. We may disable a code that is shared or misused. To cancel, or if you think you were charged by mistake, contact us at and we will look into it.

          5. Accuracy

          Food values vary by recipe, portion, and cooking method. We try to keep the information useful, but we cannot promise it is complete or error-free. Results are not guaranteed.

          6. Fair use

          Do not copy, resell, or scrape the site, try to break or bypass it, or use it for anything unlawful.

          7. Liability

          NaijaPlate is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for losses or health outcomes that result from using the site or following its estimates.
